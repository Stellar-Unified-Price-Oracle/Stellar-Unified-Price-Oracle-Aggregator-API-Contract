**Status:** Active | Issue: #883

This document defines the contribution pipeline for newcomers and designates maintainer mentors across architectural focus areas in the Stellar Unified Price Oracle contract.

## Issue Lifecycle for New Contributors

1. **Tagging**: Issues suitable for newcomers are tagged with  and an area label (e.g. , ).
2. **Mentorship Assignment**: The area mentor is automatically notified upon issue creation to provide reproduction hints and architectural pointers.
3. **Draft Review**: Mentors review work-in-progress PRs within 48 hours to offer guidance without blocking progress.
4. **Graduation**: Contributors with 3+ merged good-first-issues are invited to tackle intermediate features.
